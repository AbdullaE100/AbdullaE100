# Dubai/UAE Resident Pain Points and Unmet Needs (2024–2026) — Evidence Base for App Opportunities

Research date: 6 November 2026. Scope: Dubai first, the wider UAE second. Evidence from 2024–2026 is preferred, and older items are labelled with their year.

**Method note for the report writer:**
- Reddit could not be reached directly: the search tool returned "domain not accessible" for reddit.com. Reddit sentiment therefore appears only where news outlets quote Reddit threads.
- Google Trends, App Store and Google Play review text, and live Facebook group counts were not accessed. Those demand signals are listed as gaps.
- Most quantitative evidence comes from Gulf News, Khaleej Times (KT), The National, official bodies (CBUAE, IDF, MOHRE, RDC, Dubai statistics) and broker or industry surveys. Broker surveys have commercial interests, and this is noted where relevant.

## Q1. What do Dubai residents complain about most, and how do the problems rank by intensity?

### Takeaway
The highest-intensity problems combine high frequency, large sums of money and broad reach:
1. Housing cost and rent-renewal stress.
2. Traffic, commute time and rising Salik, parking and insurance costs.
3. General cost of living, with an inability to save and card or BNPL debt.
4. For lower-income residents, finding legal, affordable shared housing after the 2025 partition crackdown.

Diabetes and obesity affect the most people but are less acutely felt. Government admin (visas, Emirates ID) is notably NOT a major pain point, because the UAE ranks #1 globally for online government services.

### Cited Findings
**Scale of the market**
- Dubai's population was 4,807,185 in September 2026, up 226,885 since January 2026 (Dubai Data and Statistics Establishment) — [Emirates247, Sep 2026](https://www.emirates247.com/uae/dubai-population-hits-48-million-in-september-2026-up-226885-people-since-january-2026/6122); [Gulf News, 2026](https://gulfnews.com/uae/dubai-population-rises-by-226885-since-start-of-2026-1.500692623)

- Dubai crossed 4 million residents in August 2025 — [The National, 28 Aug 2025](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2025/08/28/as-dubais-population-reaches-four-million-what-lies-ahead-for-a-city-that-keeps-on-growing)

**InterNations Expat Insider 2025 (UAE overall, 7th of 46 countries)**
- Source for this group: [InterNations Expat Insider 2025 – UAE](https://assets.in-cdn.net/expat-insider/2025/uae-2025); also [The National, 2 Sep 2025](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2025/09/02/uae-expats-relocate-quality-of-life)
- Rankings:
  - 1st: Expat Essentials, online admin and government services, Economy, Safety.
  - 2nd: Quality of Life.
  - 11th: Ease of Settling In.
  - 12th: Housing.

- 79% found it easy to get a visa; 68% found housing easy to find.
- Social life:
  - 46% say befriending locals is easy.
  - Only 3% have mostly local friends (global 16%).
  - 78% feel welcome.

**Cost of living and stress**

  - 74% worry that inflation affects their ability to stay healthy.

In the same poll, 17% would "definitely" and 33% would "probably" consider leaving the UAE over costs — [The National (older, undated)](https://www.thenationalnews.com/uae/high-living-costs-force-half-of-expats-to-consider-leaving-uae)
- Mercer's 2024 ranking put Dubai 15th globally, up three places, and the most expensive city in the Arab world for expats — [Argaam, 2024](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1735806); [Gulf Business, 2024](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2024/industry/mercer-2024-cost-of-living-how-expensive-dubai). A DMCC blog cites a "June 2025" Mercer 15th place, but it appears to conflate this with 2024 data — [DMCC blog](https://dmcc.ae/blog/cost-of-living-in-dubai-for-expats?hsLang=en)

- Government admin is a poor target. Expats rate the UAE #1 for online government services and 79% find visas easy, so pain concentrates in the private-sector and grey zones: landlords, agents, employers, insurers, used-car sellers and informal housing.

- Work hours (30th) and work-life balance (34th) rank poorly. Time-saving and outsourcing propositions (home services, errands) therefore have structural demand.

## Q2. Housing and renting: rent increases, RERA index, Ejari, cheques, deposits, disputes, partitions, flatmates, moving

### Takeaway
Housing is the #1 money pain.
- The average tenant pays about Dh99,000 a year.
- 55% of tenants now plan to buy within 1–3 years, up from 25%.
- 69% would relocate if rent rose significantly.
- Rents rose steeply through 2025, and growth is now slowing to about 4–6% in 2026 as new supply arrives.

Secondary pains:
- Upfront cheques. Funded fintechs charge 4–16% to fix these, which shows strong willingness to pay.
- Withheld deposits.
- Rental scams on Facebook and Dubizzle that target new arrivals.
- A severe affordable-housing squeeze for low-income workers after the June 2025 partition crackdown.

### Cited Findings
**Rent levels and increases**
- Betterhomes Future Living Report 2025 (n=1,439 Dubai residents), all from [Khaleej Times, 25 Nov 2025](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/dubai-rent-hike-pushes-tenants-buy-homes):
  - 55% of tenants plan to buy within 1–3 years (25% the year before).
  - 69% of renters would consider relocating if rent rose significantly.
  - Average rent is about Dh99,000 a year.
  - 59% plan to stay 10+ years, with an average intended stay of 11.2 years.
- A MoveSouq survey (n=200, undated, probably pre-2024) found 82% of tenants saw an increase at renewal, and 63% planned to move at their next renewal — [Arabian Business (undated)](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/interviews/property/82-of-dubai-tenants-say-forced-pay-more-on-rental-renewal-589231)
- Dubai rents rose "up to 53%" in H1 2025 as the market showed signs of stabilising (headline figure) — [HiDubai Focus, 2025](https://focus.hidubai.com/dubai-rents-rise-up-to-53-as-market-shows-signs-of-stabilisation-in-h1-2025)
- Rent growth slowed in late 2025 as new homes gave tenants more choice — [Gulf News via Magzter, 2025](https://www.magzter.com/nl/stories/newspaper/Gulf-News/DUBAI-RENT-GROWTH-SLOWS-AS-NEW-HOMES-GIVE-TENANTS-MORE-CHOICE)
- Rents are forecast to rise "up to 6%" in 2026, with 4–6% in select high-demand areas (villas, townhouses, larger apartments in established communities). Landlord incentives are expected to become more widespread — [Khaleej Times, 29 Dec 2025](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/dubai-rents-increase-6-2026-population-growth)
- Allowed increases depend on the RERA Rent Index (how far current rent sits below comparable units). Tenants are advised to "first check the RERA Rent Index" before accepting a hike — [Cushman & Wakefield](https://www.cushwake.ae/en/news/first-rent-hike-in-years-for-many-tenants-in-dubai-should-they-be-worried)

**Disputes and deposits**
- The Rental Disputes Centre concluded 443 settlement agreements worth AED 190.7m in Q2 2025, with a six-day average for amicable settlements — [RDC, 2025](https://rdc.gov.ae/en/news-events/dubai-rental-disputes-center-concludes-443-settlement-agreements-worth-aed-1907-million-in-q2-2025/)
- The RDC concluded 2,350 amicable settlements worth Dh401m in January–September 2026 — [Khaleej Times, 2026](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/legal/dubai-rental-disputes-settled-six-days-average-2026)
- Deposit disputes recur in the "ask us" columns:
  - A Feb 2026 reader case involved a Dubai Marina tenant whose landlord withheld the deposit, claiming damage and cleaning costs, even though the tenant had move-out photos — [The National, 20 Feb 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2026/02/20/uae-property-my-landlord-is-refusing-to-return-my-security-deposit-claiming-property-is-damaged)
  - [The National, 4 Jan 2025](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2025/01/04/uae-property-i-am-concerned-about-recovering-deposit-from-landlord-after-lease-expires); [The National, 19 Jul 2025](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2025/07/19/dubai-tenant-renting-landlord-deposit); [Gulf News explainer, 2026](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/housing/dubai-landlord-refusing-to-return-your-security-deposit-heres-what-tenants-can-do-1.500694281)

**Cheques and upfront payment**
- Rent is traditionally paid in one to four (sometimes up to six) post-dated cheques. The DLD "Flexi Rent" initiative and several platforms now offer monthly options — [The National, 31 Oct 2025](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2025/10/31/dubai-rent-payment-one-cheque-landlord-laws); [Khaleej Times, Dec 2025](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/4-cheques-12-instalments-uae-monthly-rent-payments-trend)
- Tenants who move to 12 monthly instalments pay a convenience fee, and landlords often raise rates for multi-cheque payment — [Khaleej Times, Dec 2025](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/4-cheques-12-instalments-uae-monthly-rent-payments-trend)
- Monthly-rent providers and pricing, all from [The National, 20 Apr 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/property/2026/04/20/dubai-rent-can-tenants-finally-pay-monthly-instead-of-upfront-cheques/):
  - Takeem (an insurance-backed guarantee that replaces cheques with monthly direct debit) charges about 4% of annual rent. Its units grew from about 55,000 to 95,000 "in a matter of weeks", representing more than Dh9bn of annual rent; its consumer product launched in January 2026.
  - Pay-later platforms (Rently, Keyper, ezy) add about 5–16% to the cost.
  - Remaining barriers: landlords use cheques for screening and enforcement, and tenants "didn't feel urgency."
- One article claims monthly payment became mandatory for new contracts from January 2025. This is not corroborated by other sources and appears unreliable — [Asia Lifestyle Magazine, Apr 2026](https://www.asialifestylemagazine.com/dubai-rental-reform-monthly-payments/)

**Partition and bed-space crackdown (2025)**
- The crackdown followed a June 2025 Dubai Marina tower fire. Police reportedly found 532 occupied units averaging seven people per apartment (secondary source) — [2LT International News](https://www.2lt.com.au/tower-fire-triggers-mass-evictions-from-partitioned-dubai-apartments); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/dubai-illegal-partitioned-rooms-crackdown); [The National, 28 Jun 2025](https://thenational-the-national-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/uae/2025/06/28/dubai-illegal-partition-eviction-room-rent-bed-space)
- Displaced residents, from [Khaleej Times, 25 Jun 2025](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/dubai-partition-flats-crackdown-move-to-other-emirates):
  - One resident went from a Dh1,400 partition in Al Rigga to a Dh700 shared space in Sharjah, now commuting "over 90 minutes each way" to JLT.
  - Another went from a Dh800 bed space with 12 people in one room to Dh1,000 for a room shared with three others.
  - Quote: "Many of us live hand to mouth. We don't have Dh2,000 to spend on rent."
- Bed space costs about Dh900 in Karama, and some landlords charged Dh700–800 "for a mattress space", all from [Khaleej Times, 2 Jul 2025](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/dubai-tenants-affordable-housing-illegal-partitions). Quotes:
  - "Finding a room that is close to work and within budget is difficult."
  - "If it's too expensive, people will go back to overcrowding."
  - "We need a little help, something legal, safe, and affordable."
- Licensed co-living units are emerging, but they are costlier — [Gulf News via Magzter, 2025](https://www.magzter.com/nb/stories/newspaper/Gulf-News/LEGAL-BUT-COSTLIER-COSHARING-UNITS-EMERGE-FROM-THE-SHADOWS)
- Shared living is not banned outright, but it needs approval and must have no makeshift partitions — [The Law Reporters](https://thelawreporters.com/sharing-an-apartment-in-dubai-what-the-law-permits-and-what-could-get-tenants-evicted)

**Rental scams (flatmates, rooms, fake agents)**
- Victims profiled in [Khaleej Times, 1 Jul 2025](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/dubai-rental-scam-agents-deposits-disappear):
  - A 23-year-old new arrival from India lost a Dh500 "booking fee" to a Facebook Marketplace listing.
  - A Tunisian expat lost Dh300, then Dh1,000, via a Facebook group and listings.
  - A new arrival paid Dh2,800 upfront for a Dubizzle shared flat, which turned out to house six women; the agent reportedly made Dh21,200 in the first month.
  - Quote: "It all felt very urgent. I didn't want to miss out."
- Dubai Police arrested a fake agent using below-market listings in June 2025 — [Khaleej Times, Jun 2025](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/crime/dubai-police-arrest-fake-agent-scam-apartment-deals)
- A man was jailed over a Dh2,000 Facebook apartment-deposit scam — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/crime/dubai-man-jailed-over-dh2000-facebook-apartment-rental-scam-1.500347202)
- Dubai Police alerted residents to a new online rental scam — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/uae%2Fsearching-for-a-home-dubai-police-alert-residents-to-new-rental-scam-online-1.500461271)

**Ejari and Dubai REST friction**
- Common Ejari delays include landlord approval lagging in the Dubai REST app and old contracts not cancelled. DEWA will not connect without a valid Ejari — [Gigwise guide](https://www.gigwise.com/?p=47630); [Exclusive Links 2026 guide](https://exclusive-links.com/media/uae-property-blog/ejari-in-dubai-2026-common-mistakes-delays-and-how-to-avoid-them-updated-guide)
- Dubai REST is the official DLD app — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/dubai-rest/id1437805105)

**Pets**
- Building-level pet bans and short eviction notices have been reported (older, undated Gulf News) — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/pet-owners-hit-hard-with-more-buildings-imposing-bans-1.1354923)
- Platforms increasingly add pet-friendly filters (Jan 2026) — [Realty+](https://www.rprealtyplus.com/index.php/news-views/which-pets-do-renters-keep-in-dubais-changing-rental-homes-today-123917.html)
- Building and HOA rules restrict pet size and breed — [Provident Estate 2025](https://providentestate.com/blog/pet-friendly-areas-dubai)

### Inferences
- The renewal moment is the sharpest recurring housing pain. Tenants must check the RERA index, negotiate, decide whether to move, and budget for moving and deposit costs. The DLD calculator only answers "is this increase allowed". Nothing found helps tenants negotiate, forecast or compare renewal versus moving with real costs (Salik, commute, school run).
- Rent liquidity has proven willingness to pay (4% to 16% fees), but well-funded fintechs and insurers are already competing for it. That makes it a poor fit for a bootstrapped solo founder unless he partners as a distribution or comparison layer.
- The low-income shared-housing segment shows extreme pain and high frequency (frequent moves), but low ability to pay. Monetisation would probably have to come from the landlord or operator side (licensed co-living operators paying for leads) rather than tenants.
- Scams cluster on Facebook and Dubizzle at the moment a newcomer needs housing fast. That points to a trust or verification layer (permit/agent check, deposit escrow) as an unmet need.
- Deposit disputes hinge on move-in and move-out evidence, which suggests a simple condition-report tool (timestamped photos, checklist, PDF for the RDC).

### Gaps
- No primary figure was found for the share of Dubai residents who rent. Brokers' claims that it is a majority are plausible but unsourced here.
- No full-year 2025 RDC case count was found.
- No count was found of people displaced by the partition crackdown ("thousands" only).
- No App Store or Play review text for Dubai REST or Ejari was collected.
- No hard data was found on Facebook flatmate or room group sizes.
- Moving-cost data (movers, DEWA deposits) was not collected.

## Q3. Onboarding new arrivals: visa, Emirates ID, bank accounts, DEWA, telecoms, driving licence, medical tests

### Takeaway
Onboarding admin is comparatively easy: the UAE ranks #1 for online government services, and 79% of expats find visas easy. The real friction points are driving licences for non-exchange nationalities (about Dh4,500–7,000 for lessons), bank minimum-salary thresholds, and dependency chains such as Ejari before DEWA. Arrivals are numerous: Dubai added about 227,000 net residents in the first nine months of 2026.

### Cited Findings
- UAE expats rank Expat Essentials and online admin/government services 1st, and 79% get a visa easily — [Expat Insider 2025](https://assets.in-cdn.net/expat-insider/2025/uae-2025)
- Dubai's net population grew by 226,885 from January to September 2026 — [Gulf News, 2026](https://gulfnews.com/uae/dubai-population-rises-by-226885-since-start-of-2026-1.500692623)

**Driving licence**
- Sources disagree on how many countries are eligible for direct exchange: 57 per a Dec 2025 review versus 36 in other sources — [Real Estate Club Dubai, 2026](https://realestateclubdubai.com/blog/dubai-life/driving-in-dubai-license-conversion-rules-expats-2026); [Hertz UAE](https://www.hertz.ae/blog/how-to-convert-driving-licence-in-dubai)
- Others must take lessons, costing Dh4,500–7,000. Under "Golden Chance", holders of a valid licence from a non-eligible country can go straight to the tests — [Khaleej Times](https://khaleejtimes.com/uae/transport/dubai-8-ways-expats-can-get-a-driving-licence-in-the-emirate?amp=1); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/transport/dubai-three-ways-you-can-get-a-licence-without-taking-driving-lessons-1.1694529169051)

**Bank accounts**
- Minimum-salary thresholds are reported at AED 3,000–5,000, and up to AED 8,000 at some banks. One case described a new arrival on AED 4,500 who "struggled with most banks". Digital banks such as Mashreq Neo have no minimum salary. All figures come from secondary or commercial blogs and conflict — [Dubai Money Matters, 2026](https://dubaimoneymatters.com/open-bank-account-dubai-new-expat-2026/); [Real Estate Club Dubai, 2026](https://realestateclubdubai.com/blog/relocation/best-dubai-bank-account-by-salary-band-2026-fees-minimum-balance); [Homebook](https://homebook.ae/blog/open-bank-account-in-dubai)
- AECB credit scores start from zero for newcomers ("thin file"). Late telecom and DEWA bills count against the score — [Paisabazaar UAE](https://www.paisabazaar.ae/credit-score.md); [ADCB guide](https://www.adcb.com/en/consumer-education-awareness/money-guide-expatriates-uae/understanding-what-lenders-see-in-your-aecb-report)

**Utilities and visas**
- DEWA will not activate without a valid Ejari — [Gigwise](https://www.gigwise.com/?p=47630)
- Visas for job-seekers:
  - A UAE sponsor-free job-seeker visit visa (up to 120 days) was reported effective 30 June 2025 (commercial blog) — [Careerical](https://careerical.com/uae-opens-sponsor-free-visit-visa-for-job-seekers-short-term-projects)
  - Dubai introduced a single-entry job-seeker visa valid for 60, 90 or 120 days in Sep 2026 — [Gulf Today, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.gulftoday.ae/news/2026/09/10/dubai-introduces-a-new-single-entry-visa-for-job-seekers-valid-for-60-90-or-120-days)

### Inferences
- A generic "visa and Emirates ID helper" would compete against excellent government apps and is unlikely to win.
- The opportunity lies in sequencing and orchestration: a first-90-days checklist (Ejari, then DEWA, then bank, then licence, then AECB) built around grey-zone private-sector steps, with personalised cost estimates. This is a top-of-funnel acquisition tool rather than a standalone business.
- Arrivals are high-volume and recurring, so newcomer-targeted products refill their audience continuously.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 survey was found measuring newcomer satisfaction with specific steps (bank, licence, telecom).
- No App Store review analysis of ICP/GDRFA, DubaiNow or RTA apps was done.
- Gross annual arrivals (as opposed to net growth) are not known.

## Q4. Cars and commuting: traffic, Salik, parking, fines, insurance, servicing, used-car scams

### Takeaway
This pain is daily and near-universal:
- 91% of Dubai respondents face jams.
- TomTom estimates 72 hours lost in 2025, and INRIX estimates 45 hours (up from 35).
- Variable Salik pricing (since Jan 2025), Parkin peak pricing (since Apr 2025) and a 5% VAT (from Jun 2026) have raised costs. Residents report Salik bills doubling to Dh550+ a month and car insurance doubling.

Used-car odometer fraud is rare but expensive.

### Cited Findings
**Congestion**
- RoadSafetyUAE / Al Wathba survey (n=1,021, Jun 2025), all from [Gulf News, 30 Jun 2025](https://gulfnews.com/uae/90-of-dubai-sharjah-commuters-hit-by-traffic-jams-new-study-finds-1.500181486):
  - 86% of UAE respondents face congestion, including 91% in Dubai and 90% in Sharjah.
  - 80% say traffic is worse than in 2024 (85% in Dubai).
  - 83% want better public transport.
  - Causes cited: too many vehicles (65%), office start times (54%) and school start times (48%).
  - RTA counted 3.5 million vehicles on Dubai roads in daytime in 2024, and registrations rose 10% in two years.
- TomTom estimates 72 hours lost in jams in 2025. A 10 km drive averaged 19.1 minutes in 2025, versus 13.7 minutes in 2024 — [Khaleej Times, 2025/26](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/transport/dubai-commute-time-increase-2025-more-cars); [TomTom Traffic Index](https://www.tomtom.com/traffic-index/city/dubai/)

Monetisation is uncertain; lead generation to lawyers, brokers or insurers is plausible.
