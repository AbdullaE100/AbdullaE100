# Go-to-market and company setup for a solo, bootstrapped app founder in Dubai/UAE (2025–2026)

Research date: 6 November 2026. How reliable the sources are: free zones mostly do not publish price lists (IFZA publishes none at all), so many setup prices come from consultancy and agent blogs. Each one is marked as official or secondary. The shared web-search budget ran out partway through. Community channels, PR outlets, food delivery rules, medical-device software rules, SCA/DFSA, AstroLabs and Google/TikTok/Snap UAE CPIs were therefore researched thinly or not at all. These are listed under Gaps.

## 1. Company setup, banking, Apple/Google developer accounts and tax

### Takeaway
The cheapest legitimate legal entity (an LLC that Apple will accept for an organisation developer account) costs about AED 5,000–7,000 a year without a visa, at SPC, RAKEZ or Shams. With one investor visa it costs about AED 12,000–14,500. Dubai-based IFZA and Meydan cost about AED 12,500–12,900 without a visa and about AED 21,000–22,000 with one. DMCC is about AED 34,000+ and does not fit the budget. Tax should not cost anything at this stage. Corporate tax is 0% on the first AED 375k of profit. Small Business Relief (revenue of AED 3m or less) has been extended to tax periods ending by 31 Dec 2029. VAT registration is only mandatory above AED 375k of taxable supplies. Apple collects and remits the 5% UAE VAT on App Store sales. However, corporate tax registration is mandatory even at zero revenue, and registering late carries a fine of AED 10,000.

**Apple and Google developer accounts**
- **Who can enrol as an Apple organisation:**
  - The business must be a recognised legal entity (corporation, LP, LLC). DBAs, trade names and branches are not accepted.
  - "Sole proprietorships and single-person businesses should enroll as an individual."
  - Companies must provide a D-U-N-S Number registered to the exact legal entity name.
  - Source: [Apple Developer – D-U-N-S help](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/) (official).
- **Getting a D-U-N-S Number:**
  - It is free through Apple's lookup tool. D&B takes up to 5 business days and expediting doesn't shorten this. Apple then needs up to 2 business days to receive the data. Email D&B if it takes more than 2 weeks. — [Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/).
  - In the UAE the number is issued by Dun & Bradstreet (not DET, the free zone or the FTA) and costs nothing. Mismatched company details are the most common cause of delay. — [Avyanco DUNS guide](https://avyanco.com/duns-number-uae/).

**Business banking**
- **Wio Business plans:** Essential AED 99/month (10 local transfers and 1 WPS file included); Grow AED 249/month (unlimited local transfers); extra users AED 26.25/month. — [Kredit.ae summary of Wio fee schedule, Sep 2026](https://kredit.ae/business/wio-business).
- **Official confirmation:** Wio's own healthcare page also shows Essential at AED 99. — [Wio](https://wio.io/business/industries/healthcare).
- **Conflicting claims:** Some sources say Wio is "AED 0 monthly" or list Grow at AED 199. These look outdated. — [BusinessBankIndex](https://businessbankindex.pages.dev/ae/for-startups); [Noble Core](https://noblecoreventures.com/wio-bank-business-account-uae/).
- **Free zone acceptance:** DMCC, DIFC, RAKEZ, Meydan and DDA licences are reported as accepted. — [BusinessBankIndex](https://businessbankindex.pages.dev/ae/for-llc) (secondary).

**Corporate tax**
- **Rate:** 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above it. — [Middle East Briefing, Jul 2026](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-freelance-licence-visa-costs-requirements-2026/).
  - The same article says CT "applies above AED 1 million turnover". That AED 1m threshold actually concerns natural persons doing business, not companies. Treat that sentence as unreliable.
- **Small Business Relief (SBR):**
  - Ministerial Decision No. 131 of 2026 extended SBR from tax periods ending on or before 31 Dec 2026 to periods ending on or before **31 Dec 2029**. The AED 3m revenue threshold is unchanged. — [Osome](https://osome.com/ae/blog/small-business-relief-extended-2029/); [Entrepreneur ME](https://mena.entrepreneur.com/business-news/uae-extends-small-business-tax-relief-through-2029).
  - One source dates the decision 29 July 2026 (unverified).
  - SBR is not automatic. It must be elected on each CT return within the 9-month filing window.
  - The test is on revenue, not profit. Exceeding AED 3m in any period ends eligibility for later periods.
  - Qualifying Free Zone Persons (QFZPs) and members of multinational groups with revenue of AED 3.15bn or more are excluded.
  - Electing SBR means giving up loss carry-forward and interest deductions for that period. Artificial splitting of the business is barred.
  - Source for these conditions: [Osome](https://osome.com/ae/blog/small-business-relief-extended-2029/).
- **Free zone 0% regime (QFZP):** Non-qualifying revenue must not exceed the lower of 5% of total revenue or AED 5m. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/corporate-tax/uae-corporate-tax-why-uae-free-zone-businesses-must-stay-on-right-side-of-5-of-total-revenue-or-dh5-million-1.1685678193232).
  - Revenue from non-free-zone persons for non-qualifying activities counts as non-qualifying. — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/uae-corporate-tax-law-what-constitutes-qualifying-income-for-free-zone-persons).
  - One source says a breach loses QFZP status for that period and the following four. — [search-summarised advisory sources](https://cms.law/en/omn/legal-updates/uae-corporate-tax-law-what-constitutes-qualifying-income-for-free-zone-persons) (verify).
- **Registration:** All free zone companies, including QFZPs at 0%, must register for CT. The late-registration penalty is a flat AED 10,000.
  - The FTA waiver cancels or refunds that penalty if the first return is filed within 7 months of the end of the first tax period. — [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/services/tax/perspectives/public-clarification-on-penalty-waiver-released.html); [DLA Piper, Jun 2025](https://www.dlapiper.com/insights/publications/gulf-tax-insights/2025/gulf-tax-insights--june-2025/fta-announces-penalty-waiver-for-late-corporate-tax-registration).

**VAT**
- **Thresholds:** Registration is mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000, or are expected to within the next 30 days. Voluntary registration is possible from AED 187,500. The threshold does not apply to foreign businesses. — [FTA official](https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/Vat/Vat.Topics/registration.for.vat.aspx).
- **App Store:** Since January 2018 Apple has administered collection and remittance of 5% VAT in the UAE. Developer proceeds are calculated on the price excluding tax. — [MacRumors, 25 Jan 2018](https://macrumors.com/2018/01/25/apple-tax-pricing-changes-foreign-app-stores).
- **Marketplaces generally:** When electronic services are sold through an electronic marketplace, the marketplace is treated as the deemed supplier for UAE VAT. — [VATCalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/uae/uae-value-added-tax-on-non-resident-digital-services/). No source specifically confirming Google Play's UAE practice was found.

### Inferences
- **The cheapest options are not legal entities.** The Abu Dhabi freelancer licence (about AED 1,200) is a sole-establishment style licence, and freelance permits are similar. Apple says sole proprietors enrol as individuals. These routes are fine for an individual developer account but probably cannot hold an Apple organisation account (company name as seller, D-U-N-S). An FZ-LLC at SPC, RAKEZ, Shams, IFZA or Meydan is the smallest structure that unlocks the organisation account, B2B invoicing and a Wio Business account.
- **Year-one cost at the cheapest compliant level:**
  - If the founder already has UAE residency through another sponsor: about AED 5,000–7,000 (SPC, RAKEZ or Shams licence with no visa).
  - If the founder needs their own visa: about AED 12,000–14,500, plus about AED 1,000–2,000 for insurance.
  - IFZA or Meydan (Dubai address) adds about AED 7,000–9,000 a year over the Sharjah/RAK options.
- **Tax planning:** Under AED 3m revenue, electing SBR is probably simpler than relying on QFZP status. App-store revenue from UAE consumers could otherwise count as non-qualifying income. It is unclear whether a free zone person can choose not to be a QFZP so that it can elect SBR; see Gaps.
- **VAT:** Registration is likely unnecessary in year one. Apple, and probably Google, handle VAT on consumer sales. Direct web or B2B sales in the UAE would count toward the AED 375k threshold.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 tariffs were obtained from IFZA (it publishes none), Shams, DMCC, Dubai CommerCity or in5. Their figures are partner or blog estimates.
- The current status of TECOM GoFreelance and the Dubai DET freelance options in 2026 is unverified.
- Not verified here: the Apple Developer Program annual fee (USD 99 is widely known, but no source was fetched in this research), and whether Apple accepts a single-shareholder FZ-LLC as an "organisation". Apple's wording on "single-person businesses" is ambiguous, so this needs confirming directly with Apple.
- Google Play organisation account requirements (D-U-N-S and verification) were not researched.
- Not confirmed: whether a free zone company can opt out of QFZP status to elect SBR; how VAT applies to Apple/Google proceeds paid to a UAE VAT-registered developer (zero-rating or reverse charge on commissions); and Google Play's UAE VAT collection practice.
- Other banks (Mashreq NeoBiz, RAKBANK, ADCB, Emirates NBD) were not researched.

## 2. Regulated app categories: no-go and high-cost zones (licensing cost, time, difficulty)

### Takeaway
Several categories are effectively out of reach on AED 50k:
- Payments, wallets and lending (CBUAE), with SVF capital of millions of AED.
- Crypto (VARA), where application plus annual supervision is AED 120k–300k per activity.
- Gambling (GCGRA), where only one lottery licence exists.
- Clinical telehealth (DHA facility licence, licensed clinicians, UAE-hosted health data).
- Ride-hailing (RTA operator framework).
- Domestic-worker recruitment (MoHRE licence; enforcement against unlicensed platforms is active).
- Property-listing ads, which need DLD Trakheesi permits.

Media and content apps are in scope of the 2023 Media Law, and content fines run up to AED 1m. Online-only tutoring and dating apps are legal grey areas. General consumer and B2B software (habit, productivity, AI tools, non-clinical fitness) appears to need only a standard IT or software licence. Wellness versus regulated health is not settled by any source found.

### Cited Findings

**Fintech, payments and lending (CBUAE)**
- **Rules in force:** The SVF Regulation (Circular 6/2020) covers anyone issuing or operating stored value facilities. The new CBUAE Law took effect on 16 Sep 2025, and the existing RPSCS and SVF regulations stay in force until replaced. — [Cryptoverse Lawyers 2026 guide](https://www.cryptoverselawyers.io/cbuae-licensing-guide-2026/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/financial-services/1747336/uae-payment-service-provider-regulation-cbuae-rpscs-licensing-capital-and-compliance) (secondary).
- **SVF minimum capital:** Sources conflict. One gives about AED 15,000,000 paid-up plus 5% of the float; others give AED 3m–10m. — same sources.
- **Sandbox:** The CBUAE fintech sandbox reportedly offers flexibility on capital, onboarding and reporting during testing. "Single-purpose SVF" is the only automatic exemption. This comes from a single secondary source. — [Cryptoverse Lawyers](https://www.cryptoverselawyers.io/cbuae-licensing-guide-2026/).
- **BNPL providers are themselves CBUAE-licensed:** Tamara obtained a CBUAE finance licence. — [Sharikat Mubasher](https://www.sharikatmubasher.com/news/article/21512326/tamara-obtains-finance-license-from-uaes-central-bank?lang=en).

**Crypto (VARA, Dubai)**
- **Fees from the official rulebook:**
  - Advisory, and Transfer & Settlement: AED 40,000 application plus AED 80,000 annual supervision.
  - Broker-Dealer, Custody, Exchange, Lending & Borrowing, and Management & Investment: AED 100,000 application plus AED 200,000 annual supervision, per activity.
  - Source: [VARA Rulebook fee schedule](https://rulebooks.vara.ae/node/68).
- **Older 2023 items:** Licence update AED 500; withdrawal AED 10,000; token whitepaper submission AED 5,000. These may have changed. — [VARA clarification on fees](https://vara.ae/en/news/vara-publishes-clarification-and-further-information-on-fees).

**Investing (SCA/DFSA)**
- No data gathered; see Gaps.

**Health and telemedicine (DHA, Dubai)**
- **Framework:** DHA Standards for Telehealth Services v4 (ST-14), effective 26 Nov 2025. — [BusinessDubai.ae, 13 Oct 2026](https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/telehealth-licence-dubai).
- **Four licence categories:**
  - Standalone Telehealth Centre.
  - Telehealth added to an existing facility.
  - Added Booth.
  - Telehealth Platform, which is a technology tool connecting licensed facilities to patients and **cannot deliver clinical services**.
- **Costs:**
  - Adding telehealth to an existing facility: AED 2,000/yr (DHA fee sheet).
  - No DHA fee was found for a standalone centre or a platform.
  - Consultancy claims of AED 10k–30k/yr for a platform licence are unverified.
  - Source: [BusinessDubai.ae](https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/telehealth-licence-dubai).
- **Data hosting and records:**
  - Platform data must sit in a Tier-3 data centre on a DESC-certified UAE cloud.
  - Moving health data outside the UAE is prohibited without DHA approval.
  - Records must be kept 25 years and integrated with Nabidh.
  - HIPAA or ISO 27001 does not replace UAE hosting.
  - Source: [BusinessDubai.ae](https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/telehealth-licence-dubai).
- **Cost of a clinical model:**
  - A standalone DHA telehealth facility reportedly requires a DHA-licensed Medical Director at AED 200,000–400,000/yr.
  - All-in estimates are AED 30,000–60,000 for licensing alone.
  - Timelines are 4–12 weeks.
  - Sources: [DubaiBusinessServices](https://www.dubaibusinessservices.com/dubai-telemedicine-licence-2026-cost-dha-rules-timeline/) and other consultancies (secondary, conflicting).
- **Federal ICT Health Law (Federal Law No. 2 of 2019):**
  - It covers ICT use in health across the UAE, including free zones.
  - Health data cannot be stored, processed or transferred outside the UAE unless approved by a health authority or the Ministry, with exemptions added by a ministerial resolution in 2020/2021.
  - Cited fines conflict (AED 500k–700k for localisation breaches vs AED 1k–1m).
  - Sources: [Latham & Watkins](https://lw.com/thoughtLeadership/lw-new-uae-law-regulates-healthcare-data); [Al Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update-articles/setting-healthy-boundaries-regulated-exemptions-to-restrictions-on-the-transfer-the-health-data-outside-of-the-uae/); [Trowers](https://www.trowers.com/insights/ict-health-data-law).
  - Law firms note that wearables and health-monitoring apps "may be particularly affected". — [Trowers](https://www.trowers.com/insights/ict-health-data-law).

**Education and tutoring (KHDA)**
- **Conflicting views on online-only platforms:**
  - Some guides say fully online tutoring platforms need no KHDA permit, and that a free zone licence from about AED 5,750 is enough. — [UAE Free Zone Finder edtech guide](https://uaefreezonefinder.com/uae-edtech-education-technology-startup-guide-2026/) (low reliability).
  - Others say all education and tutoring activities need prior KHDA approval. — [Meydan FZ blog](https://www.meydanfz.ae/blog/start-an-online-tutoring-business-in-dubai); [BusinessDubai.ae](https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/online-tutoring-business-setup-dubai).
- **In-person and hybrid:** In-person or hybrid tutoring in Dubai triggers a KHDA tutorial-centre permit and inspection. — [UAE Free Zone Finder](https://uaefreezonefinder.com/uae-tutoring-academic-support-guide-2026/).
- **Individual tutors:** A separate UAE private tutor permit exists for individual tutors. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/uae-private-tutor-permit-who-can-apply-and-what-are-the-rules-1.500274031).

**Dating apps**
- No official TDRA statement was found. Low-reliability sources say mainstream dating apps are legal to use, but Tinder's accessibility varies or it is blocked on some networks. OkCupid and Badoo reportedly work, while Adult Friend Finder is blocked. Same-sex relationships remain criminalised. — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/uncategorised/uae-continues-to-block-dating-sites-but-allows-tinder/); [Expat.com](https://www.expat.com/en/guide/middle-east/united-arab-emirates/print-34541-dating-in-the-united-arab-emirates.html) (low reliability).

**Gambling (GCGRA)**
- **Only one licence so far:** In July 2024 the GCGRA awarded the first and only lottery licence, to The Game LLC ("UAE Lottery"). — [GCGRA press release](https://www.gcgra.gov.ae/en/news/press-releases/july-2024-press-release-the-gcgra-awards-license-for-uae-lottery/).
- **Fee structure** (no amounts published):
  - Application fee.
  - In-principle approval fee of 10% of the licence fee.
  - Licence fee.
  - Annual maintenance fee.
  - A "single digit" revenue tax on top of 9% corporate tax.
  - Sources: [iGaming Business](https://igamingbusiness.com/lottery/gcgra-uae-lottery/); [Intelium Law](https://inteliumlaw.com/gaming-license/uae/).
- **Vendor licence:** GCGRA also issues vendor licences, for example to Smartplay. — [Public Gaming](https://publicgaming.com/news-categories-m/company-news/13464-new-jersey-s-smartplay-international-receives-vendor-license-from-uae-s-gcgra).

**Media and content (Media Law and the regulator)**
- **Legal basis:** Federal Decree-Law No. 55 of 2023, with Cabinet Decision No. 42 of 2025 in effect since 29 May 2025. It applies to individuals, media institutions and platforms in the UAE, including free zones. — [Clyde & Co, May 2025](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2025/05/uae-media-regulations-framework).
- **Regulator:** The National Media Authority (NMA) replaced the Media Council as the national regulator in December 2025. — [Pinsent Masons, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/uae-influencer-licence-deadline-looms).
- **Scope:** It covers websites, social platforms, digital advertising, sponsored content and apps. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/uae-implements-new-media-law-with-penalties-up-to-dh1-million-for-violations-1.500151242).
- **Licensing fines:**
  - Operating without a media licence: AED 10,000 (first offence) or AED 40,000 (repeat).
  - Expired licence: AED 10,000 or AED 20,000.
  - Late renewal: AED 150/day, capped at AED 3,000.
  - Source: [Gulf News, updated 24 Jan 2026](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/telephone-internet/what-fines-influencers-and-content-creators-face-under-uae-media-law-1.500418948).
  - Clyde & Co says fines for unauthorised media activity start at AED 5,000. — [Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2025/05/uae-media-regulations-framework).
- **Content fines (Table 2):**

  | Violation | Fine (AED) |
  |---|---|
  | Disrespect for Islam or other religions | 100,000–1,000,000 |
  | Disrespect for the UAE's regime, symbols or institutions | 50,000–500,000 |
  | Promoting sectarianism, violence or terrorism | 100,000–500,000 |
  | Immoral, misleading or fake content | 10,000–100,000 |
  | Violating privacy or children's rights | 5,000–100,000 |

  Source: [Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2025/05/uae-media-regulations-framework).
- **Licence fee categories:** Table 1 sets separate licence fees for podcasting, electronic publishing and audiovisual production. — [Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2025/05/uae-media-regulations-framework).

**Real estate advertising (DLD / Trakheesi)**
- **Permits:** Developers and agents have needed a Trakheesi permit for property ads since 2016. Publishers who run the ads were not penalised. — [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/?p=90601).
- **Enforcement:** In October 2020 RERA fined 10 firms a progressive AED 50,000 each, for unpermitted, manipulated or expired permits. — [DLD media release](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/news-media/dld-fines-10-real-estate-companies-and-warns-another-30-for-not-adhering-to-advertising-requirements/).
- **Cost:** About AED 220 per listing permit. — [Real Estate Club Dubai, 2026](https://realestateclubdubai.com/blog/property-management/trakheesi-permit-in-dubai-what-it-is-how-to-get-it-and-why-short-term-landlords-need-one) (secondary).

**Ride-hailing and transport (RTA)**
- **Operator rules:**
  - App-based operators need an RTA Public Transport Agency permit under RTA Board Bylaw No. 130 of 2017 and Administrative Resolution No. 516 of 2022.
  - Their apps must connect to the Dubai Integrated Mobility Platform and RTA-recommended smart taximeters.
  - E-hailers work within the limousine-company framework.
  - Sources: [Al Tamimi](https://tamimi.com/law-update-articles/regulating-app-based-e-hail-taxi-services-in-dubai); [Dubai Legal Portal, Resolution 516/2022](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2022/Administrative%20Resolution%20No.%20(516)%20of%202022%20Issuing%20the%20Implementing%20Bylaw.html).
- **Drivers and vehicles:** Drivers need special RTA permits, and vehicles need limousine registration. — [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/?p=269956).

**Domestic worker recruitment (MoHRE)**
- **Licence requirement:** Recruiting or temporarily employing domestic workers without a MoHRE licence is prohibited. — [MoHRE, 23 Jul 2025](https://mohre.gov.ae/en/media-center/news/23/7/2025/mohre-and-tdra-shut-down-77-social-media-accounts-engaged-in-unlicensed-domestic-worker-recruitment).
- **Enforcement record:**
  - MoHRE and TDRA shut 77 accounts in July 2025. — [MoHRE](https://mohre.gov.ae/en/media-center/news/23/7/2025/mohre-and-tdra-shut-down-77-social-media-accounts-engaged-in-unlicensed-domestic-worker-recruitment).
  - 230 accounts were shut in 2025. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/uae-shuts-down-230-social-media-accounts-in-2025-for-promoting-recruitment-services-without-licences-1.500439532).
  - About 200 were shut in the first half of 2026. — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/uae-unlicensed-domestic-worker-service-social-media-accounts-shut-down-mohre-tdra).
- **Lawful channels:** MoHRE publishes a list of licensed recruitment offices. Sources do not say Tadbeer is the only lawful channel.

**Data protection (PDPL)**
- **Implementing rules:** The Federal PDPL (Decree-Law 45/2021) exists, but its Executive Regulations are still reported as pending and "repeatedly delayed".
- **Penalties:** AED 50,000–5,000,000, but these depend on the Executive Regulations being activated.
- **Regulator:** The UAE Data Office was set up under Decree-Law 44/2021.
- **Enforcement:** Activity is described as limited.
- **Sources:** [Chambers Doing Business 2025](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/doing-business-in-2025/842); [Baker McKenzie](https://resourcehub.bakermckenzie.com/en/resources/global-data-and-cyber-handbook/emea/uae/topics/regulators-enforcement-priorities-and-penalties).
- **Conflicting claims:** Some trackers call enforcement "Active". — [KS&K privacy map](https://ksandk.com/privacy-review/map/uae/) (low reliability).

**Categories that appear to need no special licence**
- **Software development:** It is an eligible activity under standard free zone packages, for example RAKEZ (software development, IT consulting, AI startups). — [RAKEZ blog](https://rakez.com/en/blog/details/article/187/start-your-business-in-24-hours-rakez-biz-starter-package-explained).
- **TDRA app registration:** Claims that apps distributed to UAE users need "TDRA/TRA notification registration" (one claim: AED 5,000–15,000/yr) appear only in low-reliability consultancy content. — [UAE Free Zone Finder](https://uaefreezonefinder.com/uae-edtech-education-technology-startup-guide-2026/); [DubaiBusinessServices](https://www.dubaibusinessservices.com/dubai-telemedicine-licence-2026-cost-dha-rules-timeline/). No official TDRA source was found.

### Inferences
- **How the founder's existing apps map onto these rules:**
  - Habit tracker, quit-vaping recovery, IELTS AI examiner, and an AI calorie and workout app: positioned as wellness or education software with no clinicians and no medical claims, these most likely sit under a standard software licence.
  - Quit-vaping and calorie apps: medical or treatment claims, or clinician involvement, could pull them toward DHA, the ICT Health Law or medical-device rules.
  - IELTS examiner: online-only is likely lower risk, but KHDA's position on online-only tutoring is unclear.
  - Quran learning app: it sits under the religious-content standards of the Media Law, where fines run AED 100k–1m. Accuracy and review of Quranic text matter.
- **Ideas to avoid on AED 50k** (licensing or capital alone exceeds the budget, or needs a licensed partner):
  - Wallets, payments and P2P money (CBUAE).
  - Lending, BNPL and investing advice (CBUAE/SCA/DFSA).
  - Crypto (VARA: AED 120k+ in year one for the cheapest activity).
  - Betting, lottery or prediction markets (GCGRA).
  - Clinical teleconsults (DHA facility plus Medical Director).
  - Ride-hailing (RTA).
  - Maid or nanny marketplaces (MoHRE).
  - Property listing portals (Trakheesi on every ad).
- **A workable pattern for regulated verticals:** be the "software for licensed operators", selling B2B SaaS to DHA clinics, RERA brokers, KHDA centres or licensed recruitment offices. For example, a DHA "Telehealth Platform" licence that connects licensed facilities, but note it cannot deliver clinical services.
- **Data residency:** Any app holding health data should plan for UAE hosting (DESC-certified cloud) if it touches DHA-regulated care. Generic PDPL obligations are uncertain while the Executive Regulations are pending.

## 3. Payments: gateways, fees, in-app purchase rules, BNPL

### Takeaway
Stripe is available to UAE entities at 2.9% + AED 1 for domestic cards, plus 1% for international cards and 1% for currency conversion. Ziina's published consumer-facing fees match this, although its blog claims 2.6% + AED 1 for business. Network International, Checkout.com, Tap and PayTabs quote per merchant. For digital goods inside the apps, Apple and Google billing apply. Apple's Small Business Program and Google's 15% tier take 15%. UAE is not among the markets on Google's new 2026 fee structure. Tabby and Tamara do not publish merchant rates; unverified estimates are about 4–8%. They are mainly relevant to physical goods or services and web checkout, not in-app digital purchases.

### Cited Findings
- **Stripe UAE (official pricing page, fetched 6 Nov 2026):**
  - Cards: domestic 2.9% + AED 1.00; international +1%; currency conversion +1%.
  - Billing (subscriptions): 0.7% of volume pay-as-you-go, or from AED 2,200/month on a 1-year contract.
  - Payouts: standard payouts are free; instant payouts 1.5% (minimum AED 2); USD payouts 1% (minimum $25).
  - Disputes: AED 60 per dispute.
  - Source: [Stripe AE pricing](https://stripe.com/ae/pricing).
  - Stripe's UAE promotional-pricing FAQ confirms +1% for conversion. — [Stripe support](https://support.stripe.com/questions/uae-promotional-pricing-faqs).
- **Ziina:**
  - Its blog states 2.6% + AED 1 per transaction, plus 1.5% for international cards or FX, with no subscription fee. — [Ziina blog](https://ziina.com/blog/stripe-alternatives-with-cost-effective-transaction-fees) (vendor).
  - Its fees page (updated 6 Jan 2026) lists 2.9% + AED 1 for UAE debit, UAE credit, international cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, with 5% VAT on fees. Lite plan free; Violet AED 100/month or AED 996/year. Cashouts free, including under pricing effective 1 Nov 2026. — [Ziina fees](https://ziina.com/fees).
  - The two pages conflict. The fees page may describe personal or Lite pricing rather than business.
- **Telr, Tap, PayTabs, Network International, Checkout.com, Amazon Payment Services:** These were reported in 2026 comparison pages as publishing no UAE rate card, with prices quoted per merchant. Comparison listicles' Tap and Magnati tables are flagged as matching nothing those companies publish. — summarised from [Ziina comparison page](https://ziina.com/business/comparison) and search-result comparison pages (the original page could not be pinned down; see Gaps).
- **Apple App Store Small Business Program (official):**
  - Commission is 15% for developers with up to USD 1m in proceeds in the prior calendar year and the current year, including associated accounts.
  - The page also states 10% for EU alternative-terms developers and for subscriptions after their first year.
  - Enrolment is required, and accepting the Paid Apps agreement.
  - Rates adjust 15 days after the end of the fiscal month in which enrolment is approved.
  - **Developers enrolled in the Small Business Program who have fewer than 2m first-time downloads may use Apple Foundation Models on Private Cloud Compute at no cloud API cost** once the entitlement is assigned.
  - Source: [Apple Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/).
- **Google Play (official help page, fetched Nov 2026):**
  - In markets not yet on the new structure (which by implication includes the UAE): 15% on the first USD 1m a year, 30% above, and 15% for auto-renewing subscriptions regardless of revenue.
  - The new structure (10% + 5% billing fee for subscriptions; 20–25% + 5% for other transactions) rolled out on 30 Jun 2026 in the EEA, UK and US, and on 30 Sep 2026 in Australia and Japan. No global date is given.
  - Source: [Google Play Console Help – service fees](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622).
- **Tabby and Tamara:**
  - Tabby does not publish merchant rates; they are set at application or in the contract.
  - Third-party estimates are 5–8% or about 6–8% for Tabby, and 4–8% for Tamara. These are unverified. — [CheckoutFlow Tamara vs Tabby](https://checkoutflow.pages.dev/ae-en/best-payment-gateway-ecommerce/tamara-vs-tabby) (low reliability).
  - Tamara is integrated via gateways such as Geidea, Checkout.com, Amazon Payment Services and PayTabs. — [Geidea docs](https://docs.geidea.net/docs/tamara); [Checkout.com](https://cfworker-www.checkout.com/payment-methods/tamara).

### Inferences
- **Year-one platform fees:** At under USD 1m, the effective fee on in-app digital subscriptions is 15% on both stores for a founder enrolled in Apple's Small Business Program.
- **Web payments:** For B2B SaaS or web checkout, Stripe (2.9% + AED 1) or Ziina are the self-serve options with published pricing. Stripe Billing's 0.7% adds to the cost of subscriptions.
- **BNPL:** Tabby or Tamara only make sense for high-ticket physical goods or services, such as a marketplace for services or courses sold on the web. They do not fit app-store digital subscriptions, because Apple and Google billing is generally required for digital goods consumed in-app. This last point is an inference; no store-guideline text was fetched.
- **Free AI compute:** Apple's Small Business Program perk (Foundation Models on Private Cloud Compute at no API cost) could materially cut inference costs for an AI-first iOS app.

### Gaps
- Telr's published bands (reportedly 2.45% + AED 1 down to 2.25% + AED 0.50, a 0.5% international uplift, and monthly fees from AED 249 to AED 0) could not be fetched from telr.com (404). Treat them as unverified.
- Network International N-Genius, Checkout.com, Tap and PayTabs: no published UAE rates were found.
- Not researched: Apple and Google AED price-tier specifics, whether local alternative billing or steering is allowed in the UAE, Tabby and Tamara official merchant terms, and minimum-volume requirements.

## 4. Growth channels in the UAE: platform penetration, paid benchmarks, influencer rules, ASO, PR, events, seasonality

### Takeaway
The UAE is fully online:
- 11.3m internet users; 12.5m social identities (110% of population).
- Ad reach: TikTok (12.5m adults), Facebook 9.7m, YouTube 8.4m, Instagram 8.0m, Snapchat 5.1m, X 2.9m. Reddit reaches about 22% of internet users.
- Audiences skew heavily male (about 67%).

Meta install costs in the UAE are very volatile (median about USD 0.75 to 23 a month; about USD 5.5 on average). Meta CPMs average about USD 15.8, which is below the global average. UAE ATT opt-in (53%) is above the global level. Subscription LTV (USD 21.8 median at one year) beats the US. Paid influencers must hold a National Media Authority Advertiser Permit (mandatory since 1 Feb 2026; fines up to AED 10,000), but founders promoting their own business are exempt. Install demand peaks in Ramadan (Feb–May window) and in Sep–Dec (Black Friday and Dubai Shopping Festival). GITEX and Expand North Star run 7–11 Dec 2026 at Expo City.

- **Brand obligations:**
  - Verify each influencer's permit and put the permit number in contracts.
  - Label sponsored content.
  - Health, finance, real estate and education may need additional sector approvals.
  - Source: [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/uae-influencer-licence-deadline-looms).

### Inferences
- **Choosing platforms by app:**
  - TikTok and Instagram give the broadest reach.
  - Snapchat is the most gender-balanced UAE platform (38% female) and, from GCC behaviour, likely skews toward Emirati and Arab youth, which suits Arabic-first consumer apps such as the Quran app.
  - LinkedIn's 10m members (87.6% of population) make it a realistic channel for B2B and the IELTS app, which targets professionals and migrants.
  - Reddit (about 22%) is a meaningful niche for expats.
- **Paid acquisition on a small budget:**
  - Meta UAE CPIs below USD 1–2 in Q4 and much higher around Ramadan suggest timing tests for Oct–Dec and avoiding broad paid pushes during the Ramadan or DSF CPM spikes, unless the category is Ramadan-relevant (faith, fitness and diet around iftar, habits).
  - Assume a Meta CPI of about USD 1–5 for utility apps outside peaks, and about USD 2–4 per tap on Apple Search Ads (extrapolated from US and UK medians).
  - On a budget of AED 50k (about USD 13.6k), paid spend should be a small, test-driven slice.
- **Influencers:** The founder can post about their own apps without a permit. Any paid creator must hold an NMA permit, which should be checked before paying. Health and education claims may need sector approvals.

## 5. Startup support available to a non-Emirati solo founder (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
The largest non-dilutive help comes from cloud and AI credits:
- AWS, Microsoft and Google programmes run from USD 1k to USD 350k, and UAE entities are reportedly eligible.
- Apple's Small Business Program perk gives free on-device and Private Cloud Compute Foundation Models.

Hub71 (Abu Dhabi) now takes equity. Its enhanced package is up to AED 750k: AED 250k in-kind, a AED 250k SAFE, and a further AED 250k for top performers. Dubai Startup Hub runs Co-Founder Dubai, Market Access and Scale-Up Dubai, and Dubai Chamber runs the Smartpreneur competition. Several Dubai SME programmes (Hi2, Khalifa Fund-style grants) favour Emirati founders. in5 offers incubation with workspace and licensing help, but current prices are unofficial.

- **Hub71 enhanced Company Building Program (Nov 2025):**

## 6. Case studies: UAE consumer apps and SME tools that grew organically or bootstrapped

### Takeaway
Very few documented UAE examples of bootstrapped or organic app growth with real numbers exist. The best-documented one is dubizzle (2005). It was started on about USD 12k of founder savings, ran on AED 10 a day for the first 18 months, grew one customer at a time through guerrilla marketing and word of mouth, and later sold a majority stake to Naspers. Recent solo-founder stories, such as BrainScroller (2026), give no traction data. No UAE app CAC/LTV disclosures were found.

### Cited Findings
- **dubizzle:**
  - Founded 2005 on US$12,000 (AED 44,000) of pooled savings, which had to cover salaries and development.
  - The founders lived "hand to mouth" for about three years, on AED 10 a day for the first 18 months, and did freelance copywriting for income.
  - Source: [The National, 8 Jul 2011](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/money-me-dubizzle-discovered-a-need-and-filled-it-1.423780).
  - Another profile says the founders put in USD 6,000 each.
  - They used guerrilla marketing and acquired customers "one person at a time", including cold-calling offline second-hand sellers. One less authoritative source claims 80–90% of growth was word of mouth, with AdWords a smaller part (unverified).
  - The founders sold 51% to Naspers in 2013, reportedly for USD 120m (secondary).
  - Sources: [Wamda](https://wamda.com/2013/08/dubizzle-founders-step-down-naspers-takes-over); [MENA Opportunities blog](https://menaopportunities.blogspot.com/2013/09/the-dubizzle-success-story.html).
- **BrainScroller:**
  - A micro-learning app built by a 21-year-old Emirati in Sharjah, self-taught, solo, and funded from personal savings.
  - He spent about 6 months building before having any users. Server costs were a key pressure. He plans Arabic-first localisation.
  - No user or revenue numbers were given.
  - Source: [Gulf News, 5 Jan 2026](https://gulfnews.com/business/my-business-how-a-21-year-old-emirati-built-a-mobile-learning-app-from-his-bedroom-1.500395869).
- **1clique (Dubai HR SaaS):** Bootstrapped until October 2018, then took USD 170,000 in angel money from friends. The CEO called not raising VC "a blessing in disguise". — [The National](https://thenationalnews.com/business/technology/generation-start-up-taking-away-the-hr-headache-for-smes-1.995430).
- **Cap Store UAE (Dubai SaaS, founded 2012):** About USD 450k revenue with no outside funding. These are self-reported or estimated directory figures. — [GetLatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/cap-store-uae) (low reliability).
- **Dulook DXB (government prayer app):** Nearly 1m downloads, with a 185% Ramadan usage surge. It shows organic, seasonal demand for faith apps in Dubai, but it is not a bootstrapped startup. — [Dubai Media Office, Mar 2026](https://www.mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2026/march/12-03/dulook-dxb-nears-one-million-downloads).
- **Ziina:** An in5-incubated payments startup (venture-backed, not bootstrapped). — [SME10x](https://sme10x.com/whats-the-deal/how-are-startups-like-ziina-and-podeo-thriving-at-in5).

### Inferences
- The documented UAE pattern for low-capital growth is hands-on, community-led acquisition, such as dubizzle's one-by-one seller onboarding. Combined with the UAE's above-average subscription LTV (USD 21.8) and ATT opt-in (53%), the evidence points to organic and community acquisition plus small, well-timed paid tests rather than paid-led growth.
- Faith and Ramadan-linked usage spikes (Dulook DXB +185%) support timing launches and promotions of the Quran and habit apps to the Ramadan window.

### Gaps
- No UAE consumer app with published CAC, LTV, MRR or download figures from bootstrapped growth was found. Search was exhausted before candidates could be checked further.
- Not researched: organic growth stories of UAE SME tools such as Instashop's early days, The Entertainer, Justlife, Bayzat and Wafeq.
