# Compliance beats convenience for Dubai solo founders

The best opportunity for this founder is not a consumer app. It is a mobile-first AML/KYC and permit-compliance kit sold to Dubai's **9,785 real-estate brokerage offices**. On a weighted six-criterion rubric it scored **27 of 40**, ahead of a GLP-1 mode inside TOGA (24) and a tenant rent-renewal tool (21). Brokerages face per-deal client screening and reporting duties under a 2025 AML law. They recorded **495 violations and AED 18.5M in fines in H1 2025 alone**. Yet none of seven Dubai property CRMs reviewed names a permit or AML field, and the AML tools that do exist are either generic or carry monthly minimums of roughly AED 550–1,100. Four other ideas failed validation: a Salik/parking cost optimiser, a kids'-camp marketplace, a standalone WhatsApp e-invoicing front end, and a UAE-only GLP-1 tracker. They failed for a structural reason. UAE consumer subscriptions convert at about a third of the North American rate, so at average Meta install prices each paying subscriber costs many times the USD 21.8 median first-year value. Regulation, by contrast, hands B2B tools deadline- and fine-driven demand at no cost to the vendor. The broker kit's ceiling is modest, roughly **AED 0.9–1.5M ARR** at 3–5% of Dubai offices, and it depends on founder-led selling, an unpriced competitor (azakaw) and a softening property market. The founder should therefore run five kill tests over four weeks before writing production code. The decisive one is getting **10 offices to prepay AED 199 a month off a clickable prototype by 3 December 2026**. If the tests fail, the fallback is the TOGA GLP-1 mode sold globally, not another Dubai consumer idea.

## UAE consumer subscriptions lose money on paid installs

The consumer market looks irresistible on the surface. Dubai had **4.81 million residents at the end of September 2026**, after adding about 227,000 people in nine months ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/dubai-population-rises-by-226885-since-start-of-2026-1.500692623)). The UAE leads the world in generative-AI use, at **73.3% of working-age people** ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/09/25/uae-pulls-ahead-in-global-ranking-on-ai-adoption/)). UAE in-app revenue grew **26% year on year in Q2 2025**, the fastest growth in the GCC ([Sensor Tower & Bidease](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ST_2025-Middle-East-App-Growth-Report.pdf)). Adapty puts the UAE's median first-year subscription LTV at **USD 21.8, ahead of the US at USD 19.9** ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/markdown/fastest-growing-app-markets-2026.md)).

The funnel tells a different story. RevenueCat's 2026 benchmarks put download-to-trial for the Middle East and Africa at 3.0–3.7%, and median trial-to-paid at **19.5% against 34.2% in North America** ([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)). That means roughly 0.6% of downloads become payers through a trial. UAE Meta installs averaged about **USD 5.5**, but the monthly figure swung from **USD 0.74 in December 2025 to USD 23.64 around Ramadan 2026** ([Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cost-per-app-install/united-arab-emirates)). My own arithmetic, combining regional conversion rates with a UAE all-industry install price, follows from that. A subscriber bought at the average install price costs on the order of USD 850. Even a hard paywall, which converts 10.7% of downloads by day 35 globally against 2.1% for freemium ([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)), implies about USD 50 per payer, more than double the USD 21.8 first-year value. Paid acquisition clears that bar only in the Q4 trough, when install prices fell below USD 1, and only behind a hard paywall. The regional trend is not reassuring either: developers headquartered in the Middle East and Africa are the only group with negative median MRR growth (−9.7%) ([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)).

Ceilings are low even for the winners. Flo now leads the UAE Health & Fitness top-grossing chart. Sensor Tower estimates its UAE revenue peaked at about **USD 13.5K a week** in Q3 2025 ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q3-unified-top-5-health%20and%20fitness-units-ae-600af518241bc16eb8dce802)). iOS, where premium subscribers concentrate, carries only about 22–27% of UAE mobile web traffic ([Statcounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile-tablet-console/united-arab-emirates)). The best-documented bootstrapped success from Dubai, Enrich Labs, reached at least USD 12M ARR without venture capital. It did so by selling B2B infrastructure worldwide, not consumer apps locally ([Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/a-uae-startup-hit-18-million-arr-with-14-people-and-no-vc-money/)). Outside capital is no safety net: early-stage deals took only **11% of UAE venture funding in H1 2026** ([Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2026/07/mena-startups-raise-1-7-billion-h1-2026-despite-regional-uncertainty)).

What the UAE supplies in abundance is regulation-driven demand. Government admin is a poor target because the state's own free apps are excellent: expats rank the UAE **first of 46 countries for online government services**, and 79% found getting a visa easy ([InterNations](https://assets.in-cdn.net/expat-insider/2025/uae-2025)). Consumer pain therefore sits in private-sector grey zones, and free portals or funded fintechs already serve much of it. Businesses, meanwhile, face dated obligations with real fines. SMEs that miss the e-invoicing provider deadline pay **AED 5,000 a month** ([Sovos](https://sovos.com/regulatory-updates/global-vat/uae-issues-new-e-invoicing-penalties-under-cabinet-decision-no-173-of-2026/)), and property brokers' AML failures carry fines from tens of thousands of dirhams per violation ([AML UAE](https://amluae.com/aml-regulations-for-real-estate-agents-in-uae/)). The case for consumer apps is not empty. Gulf-built apps fitze (#10), Kalee (#15) and ShredFrame (#42) all rank in the UAE Health & Fitness top-grossing chart ([Apple RSS](https://itunes.apple.com/ae/rss/topgrossingapplications/limit=200/genre=6013/json)). But they show that broad, organically grown consumer apps can earn here, not that a new niche can buy its way in.

## Six validated ideas, scored on six criteria; one clears the bar

Each idea that went through dedicated validation research was scored 1–5 on the six criteria in the brief. Willingness to pay and cost to acquire count double, because those two decide whether a founder with under AED 50k survives a year. The scores are my judgement, anchored in the evidence cited in this section and the next. Under equal weights the order at the top stays the same: 20, 18, 18, 17, 13 and 11.

| Idea and model | Demand and pain | Competition (5 = open) | Willingness to pay (×2) | Cost to acquire (×2; 5 = cheap) | Regulatory risk (5 = low) | Founder fit | Weighted /40 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brokerage AML/KYC and permit kit (B2B SaaS) | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | **27** | Top pick, conditional on kill tests |
| GLP-1 mode inside TOGA, sold globally with Gulf localisation (B2C) | 3 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 5 | **24** | Fallback; not a UAE-only business |
| Tenant renewal and negotiation pack (free tool plus referrals) | 4 | 3 | 1 | 2 | 4 | 4 | **21** | Side project at most |
| WhatsApp e-invoicing front end on a partner ASP (B2B) | 5 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 4 | **21** | Failed as standalone; reuse as a broker module |
| Kids' activities and summer-camp marketplace | 3 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 5 | 1 | **16** | No-go |
| Salik/parking/car running-cost optimiser (B2C) | 2 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 3 | **15** | No-go |

The broker kit wins because it is the only idea that combines an **enforced, recurring obligation** with **published B2B price anchors** well above what a solo product needs to charge. Its weak scores are honest ones. azakaw already targets UAE brokerages. Selling to office owners is founder-led work, not app-store discovery. The founder's existing apps contribute skills rather than code.

The GLP-1 mode scores highest on fit, because TOGA's AI calorie and workout engine already exists. Users also clearly have money: Mounjaro often costs above Dh1,700 a month, largely out of pocket ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/your-money/saving-investment/anti-obesity-drugs-ozempic-wegovy-mounjaro-are-they-getting-cheaper-1.500433563)). It scores lowest on acquisition and regulation. Federal law bans advertising prescription-only medicines to the public ([CMS](https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/cms-expert-guide-to-advertising-of-medicines-and-medical-devices/united-arab-emirates)), which removes the drug-name ads and keywords that global trackers rely on.

The tenant tool targets the city's largest money pain and has good data, but it has no evidence of willingness to pay. E-invoicing has the hardest deadline of any idea and the worst competitive position.

The research also screened several ideas without validating them, and none of these displaces the top three. A micro-employer WPS payroll-compliance tool has a real trigger: salaries have been due by the 1st of the month since June 2026, with escalation from day 2 ([Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/es/insights/2026/06/uae-introduces-stricter-wage-protection)). But moving salary files needs bank integration, so it fits better as a later module. A licence and deadline calendar is a free acquisition funnel rather than a business, and PocketLedger already sends such reminders over WhatsApp in beta ([MENA Startup Digest](https://menastartupdigest.com/?p=48919)). Pet care shows cost-driven avoidance, with over 40% of owners delaying care ([Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2026/health-care/over-40-per-cent-of-uae-pet-owners-put-off-care-over-cost-survey-finds/)), but tele-vet triage drifts toward clinical regulation. A permit-verified tutor marketplace would reuse Bandwise, but sources conflict on whether online tutoring needs KHDA approval ([Meydan FZ](https://www.meydanfz.ae/blog/start-an-online-tutoring-business-in-dubai)). Arabic-first consumer AI in the founder's Quran and IELTS niches faces little funded competition beyond HUMAIN Chat ([W.Media](https://w.media/humain-chat-saudi-arabia-launches-its-own-arabic-first-ai-app/)), but this research found no UAE-specific unmet need there.

## Four ideas failed validation, for reasons that generalise

The Salik/parking optimiser addressed a daily, near-universal pain: **91% of Dubai respondents** hit traffic jams ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/90-of-dubai-sharjah-commuters-hit-by-traffic-jams-new-study-finds-1.500181486)), and the official Smart Salik app rates **2.0 stars on iOS** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/smart-salik/id912158362)). It failed on four counts.

- **No data access.** Salik integrates only through negotiated revenue-share deals such as Parkonic's ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/dfm-listed-salik-expands-reach-outside-dubai-in-deal-with-parkonic-1.1732770271969)), and RTA's open-data page offers only PDFs ([RTA](https://rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/home/open-data)).
- **Legal risk.** Logging into users' accounts risks the cybercrime law's unauthorised-access offence, which carries **AED 100k–300k fines and/or prison** ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/uae-public-prosecution-warns-against-hacking-electronic-crimes-1.89202848)).
- **Small savings.** Salik collected Dh1.2025bn in toll fees across 2.9 million accounts in H1 2026 ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/saliks-h1-profits-drop-87-as-fines-number-of-trips-decline)). By my arithmetic that is about **Dh69 per account per month**, so time-shifting saves little. Residents already do it unaided: toll-free night trips rose 46.8% in one quarter ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/dubai-free-salik-trips-up-nearly-50-in-q2-amid-residents-tactics-to-save-on-tolls)).
- **Free clones.** Two indie car-cost trackers, MySayara and Benzeen, launched for free in October 2026 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/id6821332460); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/id6816716627)).

The kids'-camp marketplace failed on its incumbent and on cold-start economics. Kidzapp is free to parents, holds **8,751 UAE ratings** and lists 2,500+ experiences ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/kidzapp-family-activities/id1255769149)). Even so, after roughly nine years it is estimated at only about **12K monthly active users** ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.kidzapp)). QiDZ ran on advertising and commissions ([Images RetailME](https://imagesretailme.com/start-up-of-the-week-qidz)) and has since rebranded as a broader family and travel guide. Camps cost Dh550–1,500 a week ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/schools-and-parents/dubai-summer-camps-2026-essential-guide-working-parents)), but booking is seasonal and depends on signing up hundreds of providers. That is sales and operations work, the opposite of a solo data engineer's edge.

The standalone e-invoicing front end failed on unit economics, despite the strongest forcing function in the market. SMEs under AED 50M must appoint an Accredited Service Provider (ASP) by **31 March 2027** and go live on **1 July 2027** ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/ae/en/insights/tax-insights/implementation-of-the-electronic-invoicing-system-in-the-uae.html)), and 82% of UAE businesses are micro firms ([MoF](https://mof.gov.ae/en/about-us/initiatives/einvoicing/)). The costs do not work, though.

- **The ASP fee alone exceeds the target price.** Wafeq's ASP lists at **AED 89 a month or AED 890 a year per taxpayer**, plus AED 0.20–0.35 per document, and both incoming and outgoing invoices consume credits ([Wafeq](https://www.wafeq.com/en-ae/fta-e-invoicing)). For a micro firm sending and receiving 15 invoices a month, that comes to about AED 74–94 a month before any margin.
- **Incumbents bundle more for less.** Zoho Books Professional costs about AED 90 a month on annual billing, includes full accounting, 100 free e-invoices and then AED 41 per 100 ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/ae/books/pricing/)).
- **The field is crowded.** The MoF lists **86 accredited ASPs** ([MoF](https://mof.gov.ae/en/about-us/initiatives/einvoicing/einvoicing-accredited-service-providers-asps/)), and Vyapar's billing app has about 199K Play reviews ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.android.vyapar&hl=en_US)).
- **Becoming an ASP is out of reach.** Accreditation requires Peppol certification and ISO 27001 ([PwC](https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/services/tax/middle-east-tax-news-alerts/2025/ministerial-decision-no-64-of-2025.html)).

The idea revives only with a wholesale rate of roughly AED 10–25 per taxpayer, and no ASP publishes one.

The UAE-only GLP-1 tracker failed on market size, not demand. The market leader Shotsy has **89 UAE ratings against 34,573 in the US** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/glp1-tracker-shotsy/id6499510249)). No GLP-1 tracker appears in the UAE Health & Fitness top 100 ([Apple RSS](https://itunes.apple.com/ae/rss/topgrossingapplications/limit=200/genre=6013/json)), and Valeo already gives its patients symptom and dose logging free inside its prescribing app ([Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/valeo-health-redefines-glp-1-weight-management-with-a-digital-doctor-led-care-model-1202435)). The Media Council has also taken legal action over a health ad that lacked health-authority approval ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2025/09/11/uae-launches-legal-action-after-misleading-medical-claims-on-social-media-account)), and one doctor caps courses at five or six months ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/03/25/new-wave-of-cut-price-generic-weight-loss-drugs-to-heat-up-competition-for-brand-leaders-ozempic-and-wegovy)), which shortens lifetime value. The idea survives only as a feature of TOGA sold worldwide, with Gulf foods, Ramadan timing and Arabic as its differentiators.

The tenant tool survives only in reduced form. The pain is real, and the 2026 market favours tenants: in Q3 2026, **21.4% of rental contracts were signed at a lower rent than before**, and 94,300 renewals were registered in that quarter ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/dubai-rents-1-in-5-tenants-now-pays-less-than-before-as-rentals-ease-on-new-supply)). But Bayut's free TruEstimate already separates new and renewed contracts ([Bayut](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/truestimate-rental-report/)), and the research found no evidence that tenants pay for rent data. It is worth building only as an SEO tool that earns referral fees when a tenant decides to move.

These failures share three patterns: the state or a portal already gives the feature away; a platform owner controls the data and payment rails; or a bundled incumbent leaves no margin. Several categories were excluded before validation for budget or capital reasons:

- **Payment wallets.** These need a stored-value licence with AED 3m–15m of capital ([Cryptoverse Lawyers](https://www.cryptoverselawyers.io/cbuae-licensing-guide-2026/)).
- **Crypto.** Even the cheapest activity under the Dubai crypto regulator (VARA) costs AED 40,000 to apply plus AED 80,000 a year in supervision fees ([VARA](https://rulebooks.vara.ae/node/68)).
- **Clinical telehealth.** A DHA-licensed medical director reportedly costs AED 200k–400k a year ([DubaiBusinessServices](https://www.dubaibusinessservices.com/dubai-telemedicine-licence-2026-cost-dha-rules-timeline/)).
- **Maid-hiring marketplaces.** About 200 unlicensed recruitment accounts were shut in H1 2026 ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/uae-unlicensed-domestic-worker-service-social-media-accounts-shut-down-mohre-tdra)).
- **Delivery.** Keeta passed 102K weekly UAE downloads within weeks of launch ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q3-unified-top-5-food%20delivery%20services-units-ae-63da96fbe1714cfff1c1e5a1)), and InstaShop's value fell from about $360M to $32M ([Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2025/food-industry/talabat-completes-instashop-acquisition-for-32mn/)).

## Brokerage compliance is a recurring, fined and under-tooled job

The segment is large, churning and rich. At the end of 2025 Dubai had **32,294 registered brokers, 13,083 of them new that year (+38%)**, working from **9,785 offices**. They executed 96,440 transactions and earned **AED 13.59bn in commissions** ([DLD](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/news-media/dubai-s-real-estate-brokerage-sector-witnessed-a-notable-transformation-in-scale-and-impact-in-2025-reaffirming-its-position-as-a-key-regulatory-and-economic-driver-within-the-real-estate-ecosystem-this-development-was-driven-by-higher-leve/)). The buyer, however, is the office, not the agent. AML duties sit with the licensed brokerage ([AML UAE](https://amluae.com/aml-regulations-for-real-estate-agents-in-uae/)), and Dubai does not permit independent agents ([bhomes](https://www.bhomes.com/en/blog/becoming-an-agent/uk-to-dubai-real-estate-career-guide)). This corrects the earlier SME research, which priced the product per agent at AED 19–49. The real market is about 9,785 offices, averaging 3.3 brokers and roughly 10 transactions a year each (my division of the DLD totals). That points to flat per-office pricing, not per-deal fees.

The obligations recur. Federal Decree-Law 10 of 2025 took effect on 14 October 2025 ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/money-laundering/1707064/federal-decree-by-law-no-10-of-2025-regarding-anti-money-laundering-and-combating-the-financing-of-terrorism-and-proliferation-financing)). Any brokerage that concludes a sale or purchase for a client must:

- appoint a compliance officer;
- register on goAML, the government's AML reporting portal;
- screen clients against the UN, UAE local terrorist and ministry lists at onboarding, at every transaction, and whenever those lists change;
- file a real-estate activity report (REAR) for cash payments of AED 55,000 or more, or any virtual-asset settlement;
- keep records for five years.

Fines run from **AED 10,000 to AED 5,000,000 per violation**, and failures in client due diligence draw AED 50k–200k ([AML UAE](https://amluae.com/aml-regulations-for-real-estate-agents-in-uae/)). Enforcement is real. In H1 2025 the Ministry of Economy and Tourism recorded **495 violations and AED 18.5M in fines against brokerages** ([MoET](https://www.moet.gov.ae/en/-/ministry-of-economy-and-tourism-announces-h1-2025-inspection-results-on-private-sector-compliance-with-anti-money-laundering-laws)). That is roughly one violation per 20 offices in six months. Separately, the DLD fined 256 brokers in six months of 2024 for breaching advertising rules ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-land-department-fines-256-brokers-for-failing-advertising-rules-over-six-months-1.1721223064155)). Those rules require a Trakheesi permit for every listing ([PropSpace](https://www.propspace.com/blog/uae-real-estate-advertising-permits)).

The tooling gap is visible. A comparison of seven Dubai property CRMs found that **none names a Trakheesi permit field** ([Propphy](https://www.propphy.com/blog/best-real-estate-crm-dubai)). PropSpace charges AED 220 per user per month, with an AED 500 monthly minimum, and offers no AML features ([PropSpace](https://www.propspace.com/pricing)). AML tools are either generic or priced for enterprises:

- **Sumsub** charges USD 1.35–1.85 per check, with **USD 149–299 monthly minimums** (about AED 550–1,100).
- **ComplyAdvantage** charges about USD 100–120 a month and covers screening only.
- **AMLOCK** starts near USD 52,000 a year.

All three figures come from a roundup published by azakaw, a competitor that ranks itself first ([azakaw](https://www.azakaw.com/blog/best-real-estate-compliance-software)). Consultancies sell AML packages from AED 3,999 and starter plans at AED 659 a month ([Noble Core Ventures](https://noblecoreventures.com/aml-compliance-small-business-uae-2026-costs-deadlines-penalties/); [Sky Sigma](https://sky-sigma.com/services/aml-compliance)). azakaw itself is the real threat. It offers KYC, screening and "goAML-ready" reporting aimed at UAE brokerages, on usage-based pricing that the research could not find ([azakaw](https://www.azakaw.com/blog/best-real-estate-compliance-software)).

The founder's edge is mobile UX plus data work. UAE B2B compliance apps are weak on phones: Wafeq's accounting app, for example, has **three UAE App Store ratings** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/wafeq-accounting-e-invoicing/id6502498283)). The core engineering here is document capture, fuzzy name matching across Arabic and Latin scripts, and drafting structured reports. That is a data/AI engineer's home ground, and the sanctions lists and goAML itself are free ([Young & Right](https://youngandright.ae/blogs/everything-you-need-to-know-about-goaml-registration-requirements-in-uae)).

The risks are concrete:

- **A small ceiling.** At 3–5% penetration and AED 250 a month per office, ARR reaches roughly AED 0.9–1.5M.
- **A softening property market.** Dubai residential values fell a cumulative **10%** after the regional conflict began on 28 February 2026 ([ValuStrat via Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/research-studies/valustrat-dubai-residential-vpi-june-2026-dubai-residential-market-sees-softer-price-decline-and-stronger-sales-in-june-398908)), and Dubai needed about AED 2.5bn in relief packages for affected businesses ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/banking/iran-war-all-relief-measures-uae-residents-businesses-got-since-march-2026-1.500536985)).
- **Liability.** The founder is exposed if a check misses a sanctioned buyer.
- **Manual steps.** No public Trakheesi or goAML API was confirmed.
- **Uncertain enforcement.** No 2026 enforcement figures exist, so the pressure could fade.

The upside is adjacency. Precious-metals dealers drew **473 violations and AED 20M in fines** in the same half-year ([MoET](https://www.moet.gov.ae/en/-/ministry-of-economy-and-tourism-announces-h1-2025-inspection-results-on-private-sector-compliance-with-anti-money-laundering-laws)). Brokerages that invoice developers also fall into e-invoicing Phase 2, which makes that wave an upsell to an existing customer rather than a cold start.

## Four weeks of kill tests, then 30 paying offices by February

### Five kill tests decide the bet by 3 December 2026

The rule is money before code. Every test below costs little beyond the founder's time and a legal fee, and the thresholds are set so that a fail is unambiguous. They build on the validation research's recommendation of 20 interviews and 10 paid pilots at about AED 199 a month. The exact pass and kill thresholds are my design.

| # | Test | Method | Pass | Kill or pivot if | By |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pain is recurring | Interview 20 owners or compliance officers at offices that close sales, found through broker WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn and walk-ins | At least 12 do due diligence on paper, Excel or WhatsApp, and name screening or REAR decisions as a recurring chore | Fewer than 8 report recurring pain, or most say "our consultant handles it" | 19 Nov |
| 2 | Offices pay | Clickable prototype plus a founding-office offer: AED 199 a month, first quarter prepaid, refundable if the pilot build is not live by 4 January | 10 offices prepay | Fewer than 5 have paid | 3 Dec |
| 3 | No cheap twin exists | Mystery-shop azakaw, Sumsub and two AML consultancies | No self-serve broker tier under about AED 300 a month that produces goAML drafts | azakaw sells an equivalent self-serve tier at AED 200 a month or less | 16 Nov |
| 4 | A channel exists | Offer five AML consultancies a referral or reseller revenue split | At least 1 signs and at least 2 are actively interested | None is interested | 3 Dec |
| 5 | It is legal and insurable | Fixed-fee review by UAE counsel of the "workflow and record-keeping" positioning and data handling; a professional-indemnity insurance quote; confirm the goAML report upload format | Insurable, and counsel accepts the positioning | Uninsurable, or counsel says the product counts as regulated advice | 26 Nov |

If test 2 fails, the founder should not try a fifth Dubai idea. The fallback is the TOGA GLP-1 mode, sold worldwide with brand-neutral marketing.

### A narrow MVP: capture, screen, file and remind

**In scope (mobile app):** a client-onboarding wizard for an individual or company buyer, covering ID photos, beneficial-ownership questions and source-of-funds prompts. Screening against the free official lists, with fuzzy Arabic and Latin name matching and automatic rescreening when a list changes. A risk score with enhanced-due-diligence prompts for politically exposed persons. A REAR decision helper that triggers at AED 55,000 cash or any virtual asset and produces a pre-filled draft for manual goAML upload.

**In scope (web console):** the firm-wide risk-assessment template, a deal register, a five-year audit pack ready for a ministry inspection, and a permit and owner-consent (Form A) expiry calendar built from numbers the brokerage enters.

**Out of scope for now:**

- **Paid ID verification.** Defer it until revenue covers vendor minimums such as Sumsub's USD 149–299 a month.
- **Automated goAML submission and Trakheesi lookups.** Neither has a confirmed API.
- **CRM features.** PropSpace and its peers already cover them.
- **Abu Dhabi.** It uses a different permit regime.

### Setup costs under AED 15,000 and almost no tax until AED 3M revenue

An Apple organisation developer account requires a legal entity with a D-U-N-S number. The number is free and takes up to five business days, plus up to two for Apple to receive it ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/)). Apple's guidance that "single-person businesses should enroll as an individual" is ambiguous for a one-shareholder free-zone company, so confirm with Apple before relying on an organisation account. The cheapest suitable options are:

- **SPC Free Zone.** Its published Standard licence costs **AED 6,875 without a visa, or AED 14,290 with one investor visa**, and allows up to five activities ([SPC](https://www.spcfz.ae/blogs/business-license-in-sharjah-costs-requirements/)).
- **RAKEZ Biz Starter.** It costs about AED 5,499, is issued within 24 hours, and lists software development as an eligible activity ([RAKEZ](https://rakez.com/en/blog/details/article/187/start-your-business-in-24-hours-rakez-biz-starter-package-explained)).
- **A Dubai address.** IFZA or Meydan adds roughly AED 6,000 a year, with no-visa packages from about AED 12,500–12,900 ([BusinessDubai](https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/ifza-vs-meydan-free-zone)). That premium is worth paying only if brokerage owners say a Dubai address matters to them.

Tax steps follow directly from the setup:

- **Corporate tax.** Register immediately, even with no revenue, because late registration costs AED 10,000 ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/corporate-tax/uae-businesses-dont-miss-new-corporate-tax-deadline-to-avoid-fine-1.1719142291868)).
- **Small Business Relief.** This relief from corporate tax applies to revenue up to AED 3M and now runs to tax periods ending by 31 December 2029, but it must be elected on each return, and Qualifying Free Zone Persons cannot claim it ([Osome](https://osome.com/ae/blog/small-business-relief-extended-2029/)). Sales to mainland brokerages likely count as non-qualifying free-zone income, which is capped at the lower of 5% of revenue or AED 5M ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/corporate-tax/uae-corporate-tax-why-uae-free-zone-businesses-must-stay-on-right-side-of-5-of-total-revenue-or-dh5-million-1.1685678193232)). Ask a tax adviser how to secure the relief.
- **VAT.** Registration becomes mandatory at AED 375,000 of taxable supplies ([FTA](https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/Vat/Vat.Topics/registration.for.vat.aspx)), which by my arithmetic is about 125 offices on the Office tier.
- **E-invoicing.** The company will itself be a Phase 2 B2B issuer, so it must appoint an ASP by 31 March 2027. Using its own e-invoicing module would let the founder dogfood it.

### The 90-day plan and the numbers that gate it

The plan runs from 6 November 2026 to 3 February 2027. The north-star metric is **completed client files per month** across all offices, because it measures compliance coverage directly. An office that closes ten deals a year may open the app only monthly, so weekly active use would mislead.

| Window | Focus | Actions | Gate metrics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–14 (6–19 Nov) | Prove the pain | Incorporate; request the D-U-N-S number; run 20 interviews; mystery-shop competitors; build a clickable prototype | 20 interviews done; at least 12 confirm recurring manual due diligence |
| Days 15–28 (20 Nov–3 Dec) | Prove the money | Make the founding-office offer; complete the legal review and insurance quote; pitch five consultancies; confirm the goAML format | **Day-28 gate:** at least 10 prepaid offices (kill below 5); at least 1 consultancy partner |
| Days 29–60 (4 Dec–4 Jan) | Ship to pilots | Build capture, screen and file first, then the REAR helper and expiry calendar; work GITEX and Expand North Star (7–11 Dec, free visitor pass) for partners ([GITEX](https://www.gitex.com/gitex-global-2026)) | Pilot build live by 4 Jan; at least 80% of pilots activated within 7 days (risk assessment done and first client file completed) |
| Days 61–90 (5 Jan–3 Feb) | Convert and spread | Convert pilots to annual plans; give one free month per referred office; open the consultancy channel; publish content on ministry inspections and the 31 March 2027 e-invoicing deadline for brokerages; request ASP wholesale quotes | **Day-90 gate:** 30 paying offices; MRR of at least AED 7,000; no pilot churn; at least 5 offices from partners; at least 90% of pilots' new sales clients filed in the app |

The day-90 MRR target is 10 founding offices at AED 199 plus 20 offices at AED 249, about AED 6,970. On my estimate of about AED 3,000 a month in fixed costs, 12–15 paying offices cover overheads. If fewer than 15 offices are paying at day 90, stop broad selling and choose between two options. One is a white-label tool sold only through consultancies. The other is the GLP-1 fallback.

Growth should stay founder-led and close to the ground, as dubizzle's was: its founders acquired users "one person at a time" on about USD 12,000 of savings ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/money-me-dubizzle-discovered-a-need-and-filled-it-1.423780)). Four levers fit that approach:

- **Agent churn as a channel.** With 13,083 new brokers a year moving between offices ([DLD](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/news-media/dubai-s-real-estate-brokerage-sector-witnessed-a-notable-transformation-in-scale-and-impact-in-2025-reaffirming-its-position-as-a-key-regulatory-and-economic-driver-within-the-real-estate-ecosystem-this-development-was-driven-by-higher-leve/)), agents who know the app can carry it to new brokerages.
- **LinkedIn.** It reaches 10.0 million UAE members ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-united-arab-emirates)), making it the obvious B2B channel.
- **Founder content.** The founder can post about their own product without the national advertiser permit; only paid promoters need one ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/new-advertiser-permit-who-needs-faqs)).
- **No broad paid social.** Consumer install-price swings make it a poor use of a small budget.

After day 90, the aims are about 100 offices by mid-2027 and the e-invoicing module live before the 1 July 2027 go-live. Expansion to Abu Dhabi and to precious-metals dealers would follow in late 2027.

## Conclusion

The research shifts the question from "which app" to "whose deadline". In Dubai in 2026, regulators create the paid demand that consumer markets withhold, while the state's free apps and well-funded portals absorb most resident pain. A bootstrapped founder's best structure is therefore to own one compliance-heavy buyer and stack each new obligation onto that relationship: AML now, e-invoicing in 2027, possibly payroll later. That way every new rule becomes an upsell rather than a new cold start. The e-invoicing idea shows the principle clearly. The same ASP fee that makes an AED 39 standalone app impossible fits comfortably inside an AED 249 relationship the founder already owns.

For the five existing consumer apps, the implication is to treat the UAE as a localisation edge and test market, not the revenue market. They should be sold worldwide and grown organically. Apple's Small Business Program helps on both cost lines: it cuts Apple's commission to 15% and gives free access to Apple's Foundation Models on its Private Cloud Compute ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/)), which keeps AI running costs near zero. The largest unknown in this plan is not the market. It is whether a consumer-app engineer can get 30 brokerage owners to pay within 90 days, which is why the first gate asks 10 of them for money before any code is written. The evidence also has weaknesses that only fieldwork can fix: there are no willingness-to-pay surveys, no 2026 enforcement figures, no azakaw price list and no breakdown of office sizes.
